# Leave Policy

CB Nest provides three categories of paid leave to all full-time employees:

| Leave type | Annual allocation |
|---|---|
| Casual leave | 12 days |
| Sick leave | 10 days |
| Earned leave | 15 days |

Balances are allocated at the start of the calendar year. Casual and sick
leave do not carry forward; unused earned leave carries forward up to a
maximum of 10 days into the next year.

## Applying for leave

1. Planned leave must be requested at least 2 working days in advance
   through the HRMS leave module, with a reason (minimum 3 characters).
2. Every request requires manager approval before it takes effect. The
   balance is deducted only when the request is approved.
3. A new request cannot overlap the dates of any of your existing pending
   or approved requests — cancel or resolve the earlier request first.
4. Emergency sick leave must be reported to your manager before 10 AM on
   the day of absence (see the Sick Leave Policy for details).
5. Half-day leave is supported for all three leave types — see the
   Half-Day Leave Policy for the rules.

Exceptions: leave during the notice period requires HR approval in
addition to manager approval. Unpaid leave beyond exhausted balances is
granted case-by-case by HR.
