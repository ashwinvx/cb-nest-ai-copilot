# Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home with prior manager approval. WFH is a
flexibility benefit, not an entitlement, and approval is at the
manager's discretion based on role suitability and team coverage.

## Rules and conditions

1. Regular WFH is limited to 2 days per week. Additional days require
   explicit manager approval per instance.
2. WFH requests must be submitted to your manager at least 1 working day
   in advance. Same-day requests are approved only for emergencies.
3. Employees in their first month of onboarding are expected to work
   from office unless their manager approves otherwise.
4. WFH days are full working days: standard clock-in rules apply
   (see Attendance and Lateness Policy), and you must be reachable on
   company-approved communication channels during working hours.
5. Sensitive company data accessed while working remotely must follow
   the Data Security Policy: approved devices only, no shared or public
   machines.
6. WFH is not a substitute for leave. If you are unwell or unavailable
   for part of the day, file sick or half-day leave instead.
7. Managers may pause WFH arrangements during critical delivery periods
   with reasonable notice.
