# Attendance and Lateness Policy

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Clock-in should be completed in the HRMS before 9:30 AM on working days.

## Lateness

1. A grace period applies until 9:45 AM. Clock-ins between 9:30 and
   9:45 AM are recorded but not counted as late.
2. Clocking in after 9:45 AM counts as a late arrival. Up to 3 late
   arrivals per calendar month are excused without action.
3. From the 4th late arrival in a month, each further late arrival may
   be regularized by HR as a half-day casual leave deduction, after a
   discussion with your manager.
4. Arriving after 12:00 PM without an approved half-day (FIRST_HALF)
   leave request is treated as an unplanned absence for the morning and
   should be regularized with a half-day leave request the same day.
5. Repeated unexcused lateness (3 consecutive months with deductions)
   is escalated to HR under the Code of Conduct.

## Exceptions

Late arrivals caused by approved WFH transitions, medical appointments
notified in advance, or company travel are excused. If you anticipate
being late, inform your manager as early as possible — proactive
notification is always taken into account before any deduction.
