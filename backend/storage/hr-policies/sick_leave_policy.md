# Sick Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Sick leave does not carry forward and is not encashable.

## Rules and conditions

1. Unplanned sick leave must be reported to your manager before 10 AM on
   the day of absence, and the leave request should be filed in the HRMS
   as soon as reasonably possible.
2. A medical certificate is required for 3 or more consecutive days of
   sick leave, and must be attached when HR requests it.
3. Half-day sick leave is allowed (first half or second half of a single
   day) and deducts 0.5 days from the balance.
4. Sick leave cannot be combined with earned leave in a single request;
   file separate requests.
5. If the sick leave balance is exhausted, additional sick days are
   deducted from casual leave, then earned leave, with manager approval.
6. Extended illness beyond 10 consecutive working days moves to the
   extended medical leave process handled directly by HR.

Misuse of sick leave (e.g. a pattern of sick days adjoining weekends
without documentation) may be reviewed under the Code of Conduct.
